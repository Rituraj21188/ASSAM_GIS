Land Use & Land Cover (LULC) Guide

LULC datasets combine physical satellite earth observations with functional socioeconomic land management classifications.

1. Land Cover (Biophysical Form):

What Earth observation sensors physically detect on the terrain: dense forest canopy, perennial river channels, braided sandbars, and bare earth.

2. Land Use (Anthropogenic Function):

How humans exploit and manage that physical cover: commercial tea plantations, intensive paddy agriculture, and urban infrastructure.

Significance for Assam:

Critical for monitoring shifting Brahmaputra river chars, wetland flood retention capacities, agricultural acreage, and forest buffer corridors.
