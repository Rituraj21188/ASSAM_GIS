Terrain Classification Guide

🌲 Dense Forest / Canopy: Evergreen and semi-evergreen reserves, national parks (Kaziranga, Manas, Dibru-Saikhowa), and hill tract biomass.
🍃 Tea Estates & Agro: Organized tea plantation estates across Upper Assam and North Bank agroforestry belts.
🌾 Cropland / Agri: Paddy fields (Sali, Ahu, Boro), flood-receding agriculture, and fertile alluvial plain croplands.
🌊 Water Bodies: Main Brahmaputra & Barak river channels, perennial tributaries, oxbow lakes (beels), and wetlands.
🏢 Urban / Built-up: Municipal infrastructure, rural settlements, commercial hubs (Guwahati, Dibrugarh, Jorhat, Silchar).
🏖️ Chars & River Sandbars: Active alluvial sandbars, seasonal river islands, and erosion-prone deposition stretches.
